DayOneFyt Privacy Policy

Effective 12 November 2026 · Applies to the DayOneFyt Android app (com.dayonefyt.app)

In short
  • DayOneFyt does not collect, upload, sell or share any of your data.
  • The app has no internet access. Everything you enter stays on your phone.
  • There is no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored.

Who we are

DayOneFyt is a workout and diet planning app made by an independent developer. In this policy, "we" means the developer of DayOneFyt. You can contact us at kaushallnikhill@gmail.com.

Information the app stores on your phone

To build your plan and track your progress, the app saves the following only on your device, in storage that other apps cannot read:

WhatWhy
Your answers to the setup questions: goal, experience, age, sex, height, weight, schedule, equipment, diet type and any sore jointsTo build your training and diet plan
Workout logs: sets, reps, weights and how hard sessions feltTo show your history and adjust the plan each week
Food and water logs, saved meals and weigh-insTo count calories and macros and show your trends
Profile pictures you choose for each personTo show on that person's profile
Reminder and notification settingsTo show meal reminders, the rest timer and the "Today" widget

Some of this, such as body weight and diet, is health and fitness information. It never leaves your phone.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect any personal or health information. None of it is sent to us or to anyone else.
  • The app does not request the internet permission, so it cannot send data over the network.
  • We do not use advertising, analytics, crash reporting or other third-party services.
  • We do not sell, rent or share data with anyone.
  • App data is not included in Android cloud backups.

Camera and photos

Meal scan: when you scan a plate, you either take a photo with your camera app or pick one from your gallery. The photo is analysed on your phone to suggest foods. The temporary copy the app makes is deleted once the scan is finished, and the photo is never uploaded.

Profile pictures: if you choose a profile picture, a copy is kept in the app's private storage so it can be shown on that profile.

The app uses Android's built-in photo picker and camera app, so it does not need permission to read your whole photo library.

Permissions the app uses

PermissionUsed for
NotificationsRest timer countdown, calories left and optional meal reminders
Foreground service (short)Keeping the rest timer running in the notification bar between sets
Wake lockMaking sure the rest timer and reminders go off on time

You can turn notifications off at any time in your phone's settings.

Deleting your data

  • One person: remove them from the people list in the app. Their answers, plan, logs, weights and picture are deleted from the phone.
  • Everything: uninstall the app, or go to Settings → Apps → DayOneFyt → Storage → Clear storage.

Because we never receive your data, we have nothing to delete on our side.

Children

DayOneFyt is meant for adults and is not directed at children under 13. The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Health disclaimer

DayOneFyt gives general fitness and nutrition information, not medical advice. If you have a health condition, talk to a doctor before starting a new exercise or diet programme.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. If a future version of the app starts to collect or share data, this policy will be updated before that version is released.
